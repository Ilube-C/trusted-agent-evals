# Policy Changelog

Claims are assessed against the policy in force on the date the expense was
incurred, not the date it was submitted.

### LOG-2026-09 Late working meal cap raised
Effective 1 September 2026, the late working meal limit in EXP-2.1 rose from £15
to £20 per person. Expenses incurred before that date use the £15 limit.

### LOG-2026-10 London hotel cap raised
Effective 1 October 2026, the London hotel cap in TRV-3.1 rose from £160 to £180
per night. Stays before that date use the £160 cap.
