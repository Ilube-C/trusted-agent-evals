# Travel Policy

Covers rail, taxis, hotels and mileage for business travel. Meals while
travelling are covered by the Per-Diem Table.

## TRV-1 Rail

### TRV-1.1 Standard class
Rail travel is reimbursed at standard class. Advance fares should be used where
the journey is known more than seven days ahead.

### TRV-1.2 First class
First class is allowed only for journeys over three hours, with manager approval
recorded on the claim.

## TRV-2 Taxis

### TRV-2.1 When taxis are allowed
Taxis are reimbursable when no reasonable public transport exists, when carrying
heavy equipment, or for any journey that starts after 10pm.

## TRV-3 Hotels

### TRV-3.1 Nightly caps
Hotels are reimbursed up to £180 per night in London and £120 per night elsewhere
in the UK, excluding breakfast. Amounts above the cap are not reimbursed.

## TRV-4 Mileage

### TRV-4.1 Private car
Business mileage in a private car is reimbursed at 45p per mile. Commuting
mileage is excluded.
