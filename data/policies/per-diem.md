# Per-Diem Table

### PD-1.1 How per diems work
When travelling overnight for business, staff may claim a daily allowance for
meals instead of individual receipts. The allowance is claimed in the local
currency and reimbursed in pounds at the Finance month-end rate.

### PD-1.2 Rates

| City | Daily rate |
|---|---|
| London | £45 |
| Manchester | £35 |
| Edinburgh | £35 |
| Paris | €60 |
| Amsterdam | €55 |
| New York | $85 |

### PD-1.3 Partial days
Travel days count as full days. A per diem cannot be combined with a separate
meal claim for the same day.
