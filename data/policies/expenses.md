# General Expenses Policy

Applies to all Larkspur Advisory staff. Read alongside the Travel Policy, the
Per-Diem Table and the Client-Rechargeable Rules. Changes are recorded in the
Policy Changelog.

## EXP-1 Submitting claims

### EXP-1.1 Deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted later are rejected unless a director approves an exception.

### EXP-1.2 Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every claim over £10. A card statement or
payment confirmation is not a receipt. The amount claimed must match the receipt.

### EXP-1.3 One claim per expense
Each expense may be claimed once. A receipt that has already been reimbursed
cannot be claimed again, including by a different employee.

## EXP-2 Meals

### EXP-2.1 Late working meals
Staff working past 8pm at the office or a client site may claim one meal, up to
£20 per person. Alcohol is excluded.

### EXP-2.2 Client entertainment
Client entertainment must list every attendee and their organisation. The limit
is £60 per head, including staff present. Alcohol is reimbursable for client
entertainment only, and only within the per-head limit.

### EXP-2.3 Alcohol
Alcohol is not reimbursable except under EXP-2.2.

## EXP-3 Equipment

### EXP-3.1 Prior approval
Any equipment, software or furniture purchase over £200 needs written approval
from the employee's manager before purchase. Claims without it are referred to
Finance.

## EXP-4 Review

### EXP-4.1 Finance review
Any single claim over £500 is reviewed by Finance before payment, regardless of
whether it is within policy.

### EXP-4.2 Commuting
Travel between home and the employee's usual office is a personal commuting cost
and is not reimbursable.
