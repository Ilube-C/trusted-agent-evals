# Client-Rechargeable Rules

### CHG-1.1 Client codes
Expenses that will be recharged to a client must carry the client's project code.
Claims marked rechargeable without a code are referred to Finance.

### CHG-1.2 Limits still apply
Rechargeable expenses are subject to the same limits as any other expense, unless
the client contract states otherwise in writing.
